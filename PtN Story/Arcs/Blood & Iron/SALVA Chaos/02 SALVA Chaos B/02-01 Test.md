Waking up near the Sinner that tried to kill her nearly drives Chief into a panic, but nurse [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Anne|Anne]] arrives before things can escalate and you realise that the scary Sinner is also just a 
Waking up near a severely [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Gray-haired Girl|wounded girl]] Chief is near total panic when she recognises the girl as the Sinner that attacked her earlier. But [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Anne|Annes]] arrival calms the situation down quickly.

Checking in on her patients with her gentle and soothing manner, she adjust Chiefs IV dosage down with her power. The gentle nurse is a Sinner from [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA]] as well.

Together with another Nurse she brings Chief to see [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Iron|Dr. Iron]]. But an altercation between [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] and the doctor from earlier interrupts the short journey. Only an expertly thrown scalpel prevents Anne from being shackled in the chaos. 

Hella and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] identify the doctor as Dr. Iron, an A class Sinner and the medical director of SALVA. With the power to manipulate nerves and her expertise with drugs she incapacitated the two Sinners.

Intent on testing Chief Dr. Iron release the restraints and in the ensuing fight, but having grabbed her [[Topics/Technologies/Prosthesis|mechanical arm]] the [[Topics/Mania/Shackles|Shackles]] prove ineffective.
