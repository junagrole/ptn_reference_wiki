In high spirits after beating up gang members [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] mocks Chief for being so guarded around her. But gets coldly rebuked by [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] threatening her that if Chief dies, Hecate will kill her as well.

While the gangs fight every summer for the title of "[[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicate Boss]]", this time a [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Gray-haired Girl|Gray-haired girl]] is driving them into a [[Topics/Mania/Mania|Mania]] fuelled frenzy. Evidently with the young [[Topics/Mania/Sinner|Sinner]] directly targeting Chief their cover has been blown. Is there a traitor?

Several gunshots from an alleyway force the severely wounded girl to retreat. With the gang converging on the teams position they have no choice but to follow the stranger calling out to Chief.

Introducing himself as [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ted|Ted]], a [[Organisations/Security Forces/Public Security Bureau|PSB]] informant and the teams designated support, as evidenced by a call to [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Nightingale|Nightingale]]. Visibly enraged he accuses Chief of being ignorant of Syndicate. Where Sinners are spreading Mania contamination everywhere.
