According to [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ted|Ted]] [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA Hospital]], an organisation of Sinners providing refuge to [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]], is pursuing the MBCC team, leaving them little time. But before he can contact the [[Organisations/Security Forces/Public Security Bureau|PSB]] the [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|Legion]], led by [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] in person, crashes the scene with a heavy tank. With the security precinct already fallen the team can only retreat into the sewers.  

Surprised by the speed his most hated enemy has found them with, Ted suggest to link up with his forces and attack SALVA before the Legion figures out your target. [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] argues with him, but the final choice rests with Chief. Focused on hunting Subject R and answers she overrules Hecates concerns and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hellas]] warning about trusting gangsters.
