After a tough fight [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ted|Ted]] claims that the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA]] doctors were gangsters before becoming doctors. He shoots an injured [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Laura|nurse]] to force a distraught [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Brand|doctor]] to give him intelligence about [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]].
Panicked the doctor reveals that the head nurse [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Anne|Anne]] send the [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|fugitive Sinner]] into the basement at SALVA.
The only other thing he knows is that she was talking about an [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] while half conscious. Ted is exuberant at the mention of the Inheritance, which he describes as the source of [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicates]]mania crisis.

Finding out that with [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Iron|Dr. Iron]] the doctors couldn't notify [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] about it, Ted shoots Laura. But at the last moment [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] and [[Topics/Creatures/Mania/Nightmare|Nightmare]] divert the bullet. Ted argues with you that they need to die to prevent intel getting back to SALVA.

The arguments gets interrupted by a grenade hitting the ambulance causing a swarm of 
[[Topics/Creatures/Corruptors|Corruptors]] the doctors have been gathering to attack you.

Ted explains that [[Topics/Mania/Maniacs|Maniacs]] will turn into Corruptors and that SALVA is studying the process to obtain the power of [[Topics/Mania/Mania|Mania]]. Which causes an argument between him and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]]. To prevent a fight with Teds men from breaking out you stop Hella with your [[Topics/Mania/Shackles|Shackles]]. This predictably enrages Hella and baffles Ted.

The group leaves the frantic doctor and the dying nurse behind and join up with Teds men.
