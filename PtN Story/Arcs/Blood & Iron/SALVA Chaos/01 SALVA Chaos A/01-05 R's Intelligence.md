To get intelligence on [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]] from the [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Brand|senior doctor]] [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ted|Ted]] shoots an injured [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Laura|Nurse]]. The group learns that the fugitive Sinner is hidden by head nurse [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Anne|Anne]] in the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|clinics basement]]. While barely conscious the Sinner talked about an [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]]. Since [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Iron|Dr. Iron]] is currently away they couldn't inform [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] about the Sinner.

According to Ted the doctors are also just gang members and the Inheritance is the ultimate source of Mania in [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicate]]. SALVA is studying how [[Topics/Mania/Maniacs|Maniacs]] turn into [[Topics/Creatures/Corruptors|Corruptors]] to obtain the power of Mania. 

Having outlived her usefulness Ted shoots Laura, the injured nurse, to prevent word from getting back to SALVA. But [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] and [[Topics/Creatures/Mania/Nightmare|Nightmare]] deflect the bullet. Before tensions can escalate the ambulance is destroyed by a grenade releasing all the corruptors the doctors have gathered.

With the arrival of Teds gang a new conflict breaks out, to prevent it from turning into a fight, Chief restraints [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] with the [[Topics/Mania/Shackles|Shackles]]. With an enraged Hella and a baffled Ted in tow the team joins up with the gang, leaving the frantic doctor and the dying nurse behind.
