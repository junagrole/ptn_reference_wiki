The MBCC team gets drawn into the conflict between [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion]] and the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District]] gang alliance backed by [[Parma]]. With the support of corrupt [[Public Security Bureau|PSB]] brass Parma established a [[Mania Weapon]] factory in the [[Rust]] which was moved into the [[Crypt]] in early N.F. 112.
Using an [[Inheritance]] at its core the facility refined [[Mania Crystal|Mania Crystals]] extracted from [[Sinner|Sinners]] into Mania Weapons. 

By chance the Legion Sinner [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo]] overheard officers involved in the conspiracy while in PSB custody. When [[Subject R]] staged the [[MBCC Riot]] in August N.F. 112 Horo used the chance to escape and sent word to her commander [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya]]. Mortally wounded while infiltrating the Crypt she was rescued by [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Crache]] and brought to [[SALVA Hospital]]. From where she later escaped to protect the Inheritance until Zoya could come.

With the Crypt guarded by an alliance of all major Syndicate gangs ([[Organisations/Gangs/Yagyu]], [[Organisations/Gangs/Red Falcon]], [[Organisations/Gangs/Gonzales]], [[Organisations/Gangs/Carnivore]] and [[Organisations/Gangs/Thou Voodoo]]) and the lower levels severely contaminated by the Inheritance Zoya was in dire need of reliable protection. Trough her connections to [[Whitestone Industries]] she acquired a [[White Cube]] to seal the Inheritance and intel about Chief. 

Finding herself kidnapped by the Legion, forced to put her [[Shackles]] on Zoya and threatened with an implanted mania bomb by [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl]], Chief stages her own kidnapping by Yagyu to get away. Together with [[Hella]] and [[Hecate]] she discovers that Parma has been experimenting on [[Ninety-Nine]], Hellas friend, to produce advanced mania weapon. In a race against Zoya and the gangs to reach the Inheritance first they encounter Horo trapped in the lower levels.

Both parties eventually reach the Inheritance. But the whole incident was a maliciously designed trap. The gang members and stashed Mania Weapons were only fuel to awaken the Inheritance. With Zoya as the final piece to create a [[Black Ring]]. 
Teaming up with the MBCC Zoya manages to resists the temptation of power and seals the Inheritance in the White Cube.

The PSB reacts to the preliminary [[Organisations/Security Forces/MBCC|MBCC]] report [[Nightingale]] submitted by offering talks about the [[West District Management Regulations]] to Zoya herself. With the lead to Subject R cold, thanks to the bad intel that made them track Horo instead, Chief decides to stay in Syndicate until the talks are finished.
