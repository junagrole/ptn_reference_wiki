Chief using the [[Topics/Mania/Shackles|Shackles]] to restrict [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] terrifies [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]]. But [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl's]] mania bomb doesn't explode, apparently he has to manually trigger it. The relief is short lived though.

Under the influence of the [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] the Shackles begin to weaken, it urges Zoya to break free and devour Chief. Zoya accuses her of wanting to use the Inheritance herself, but is rebuffed by [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Chief|Chief]].
> [!cite] Chief
> There is something that I can't forgive, so I have to fight it.
> Mania has brought about countless misfortunes. No one should be allowed to use it to make bigger disasters. I dread it.

As [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|Horo]] predicted earlier, Zoya applauds her backbone and integrity.

Contamination takes on the form of Horo and tries to straggle Chief, demanding Zoyas release. Watching her struggle to hold on Zoya makes her choice and joins the fray at Chiefs side. No longer restrained but enhanced by the Shackles Zoya destroys the contamination capturing the team in seconds.

Carrying the team and Horo to safety, Zoya admires Chiefs courage. Horo, writhing in agony is finally freed from the control of the Inheritance, collapsing into Zoyas arms.
Confident that Horo will make it trough this, Zoya turns her attention to the altar.
