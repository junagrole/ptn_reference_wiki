To convince [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|Horo]] Chief slightly twists the story, so that instead of the [[Organisations/Security Forces/MBCC|MBCC]] they were with [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA Hospital]] and [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] all along. Send down to rescue Horo by [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl]] after [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] went down trough the trapped central elevator.

The mention of, *her brother*, Earl being concerned for her finally sells the story to her.  To make up for attacking you she throws herself into the fight against the approaching Corruptors. To [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hellas]] great bemusement the proud girl proclaims herself a honourable fighter and immediately asks you to accompany her to Zoya.

Chief trying to convince her to head to the [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] first leads to a tantrum, but her brutal injuries finally catch up with her as she collapses. Not only the fresh wounds, the fight also opened up the old wounds SALVA had provisionally treated, confirming that she is indeed the Sinner they have been chasing. Deciding against shackling her, the team takes her with them as they go deeper into the cavern.
