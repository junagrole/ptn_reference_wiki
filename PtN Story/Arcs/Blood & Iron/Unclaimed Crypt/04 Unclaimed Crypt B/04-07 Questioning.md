Carrying [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|Horo]] on her back Chief begins to question the young Sinner that had just come to again. 

At the beginning of N.F.112 Horo was lured into a police office and arrested. When they found out she was a Sinner they transferred her to MBCC where she was for about half a year. She wasn't involved in the [[Incidents/MBCC Riot|MBCC Riot]], just too advantage of it to escape.

While Horo was detained by the [[Organisations/Security Forces/Public Security Bureau|Public Security Bureau]] she overheard a conversation.

> [!cite] Horo
> He said... The [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] in the Rust has been moved, to a disused pit in the southern desert... It continues to produce forces... and they have to deal with [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] as soon as possible...

That's why she escaped and went straight to the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/Crypt|Crypt]] to verify the intelligence. There she saw someone experimenting on humans at the Stone Altar, trying to create [[Topics/Mania/Mania Crystal|Mania Crystal]].
While the Researcher was afraid the Legion would come for them before they were ready, [[Characters/Underground/Parma|Parma]] suggested to use the Inheritance itself to take them out. With the gangs themselves as bait and fuel for the Inheritance.

> [!cite] Parma
> The **Inheritance** exists for the sole purpose of granting wishes.

Unfortunately Horo was discovered and gravely wounded, but was rescued by [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Crache|Crache]]. Crache came here in search of green haired gambler, a little kid about her age, *presumably*[[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Roulecca|Roulecca]], that came here for revenge. Finding the near death Sinner instead she brought her to [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA Hospital]], where [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Dr. Iron|Dr. Iron]] managed to save her life.

The whole rumour of a MBCC escapee stealing a mania inducing power was born from Horo trying to gather intelligence on the Inheritance. As [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] summaries, it becomes clear that this was never a lead to [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]], just bad intel. But as [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] points out, finding out the truth about the Inheritance is also their job. So they have to continue regardless, even if Chief is struck by the revelation that she risked her life for a false lead.

Barely beating back the contamination the dispirited team gets a lecture from the recently awoken Horo. The Inheritance is being activated again, if it's not stopped, everyone is going to die. There is no time for second thoughts.
