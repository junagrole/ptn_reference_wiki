At [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] base camp the team can finally reach [[Organisations/Security Forces/MBCC|MBCC]] after **47 hours without contact**.
[[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Nightingale|Nightingale]] starts an investigation into the [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] and arranges a pick up for the team.

Three hours before the arranged time Chief has a final talk with [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]]. Amused at her indignation about [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl's]] bomb, Zoya informs Chief that his ability only lasts for 24 hours.
To her surprise Chiefs learns that Earl and [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|Horo]] are siblings, with his little sister still unconscious Earl will be absent for now. Even with the decontamination the corruption caused by the Inheritance got in deep.

With the lead to [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]] being dead now, [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Chief|Chief]] is determined to root out the conspiracy behind the Inheritance. But Zoya warns her, she already lost someone close that tried to investigate. To her there is no hope for [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicate]], even if they get the mastermind, the seeds have already taken root long ago. A statement that leaves Chief very concerned that Zoya might still aim to destroy it all.

But Nightingales arrives ahead of schedule, visibly relieved her Chief is safe. She clarifies to Zoya that MBCC is not here to apprehend Legion members. As a show of gratitude she only brought along minimal guards. After MBCC submitted the preliminary report to the city, the [[Organisations/Security Forces/Public Security Bureau|Public Security Bureau]] requested a meeting with Zoya to discuss the Inheritance and the future of the [[Incidents/West District Management Regulations|West District Management Regulations]].

Though sceptical Zoya agrees to a meeting, while she keeps hold of the sealed Inheritance.

After the MBCC helicopter left, [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Che|Che]] comes out of hiding to collect payment from Earl for intel provided. The reason he helped the team escape was to spy on them during the escape. 
Earl is curious why the price is uncharacteristically low. [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Bai Yi|Bai Yi]] lost the [[Topics/Technologies/White Cube|White Cube]] and [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/K.K.|K.K.]] in the operation. But Che only deflects the question, the cube was meant to used for this and one employees less is also one paycheck less, even asking for a good review for [[Organisations/Companies/Whitestone Industries|Whitestone Industries]]. With Earl clearly buying none of it, Che reveals that K.K. is gonna be safer with MBCC. While they managed to seal the Inheritance, this is just the beginning.

Still bothered by [[Characters/Unknown/Subject R|Subject R]], Chief decides to stay in Syndicate until the negotiation is over, while Nightingale finds out who fed them bad intel.

Ready to move on the Legion sets the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/Crypt|Crypt]] on fire, destroying all [[Topics/Mania/Mania Weapon|Mania Weapon]] and [[Topics/Mania/Mania Crystal|Mania Crystals]]. Horo and the retrieved documents are brought into a safe house. Zoya is determined to follow up on the lead Horo risked her life for. To finally change Syndicate.
