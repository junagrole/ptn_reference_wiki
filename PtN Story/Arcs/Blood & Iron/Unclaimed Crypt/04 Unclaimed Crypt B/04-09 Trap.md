The gang members are using guns embedded with [[Mania Crystal|Mania Crystals]], causing contamination to spread rapidly trough [[Horo|Horos]] arm from a bullet wound. Way faster than it normally would.

Both the Mania field and the contamination of [[Mania Weapon]] are being amplified by the activation of the [[Inheritance]]. Sludge is visibly oozing out from the stone altar and the ground is shaking.

Already contaminated by [[Mania]] the gang members believe the Inheritance on their side, a benevolent gift from the patron to take on [[The Legion]] and become the true masters of [[Syndicate]]. But it is all a lie, they are just fuel for the Inheritance, but even more the whole battle for supremacy is a farce. Organised by the arms dealers behind the scene, the true masters of the West District.

But to the gang members only the power of the Inheritance is important, a power they want in their possession at any cost. Even the danger of getting corrupted is getting twisted into a chance to become a powerful [[Sinner]]. Power is everything for gangs of Syndicate.

The more frenzied the gang members become the more alive the Inheritance, almost like a beating heart. [[Hella]] is starting to hear voices in her head and [[Hecate]] is terrified for Chief who experiences it as if hands were tearing at her very soul. Stunned they can't stop the gang members from rushing the altar, causing Horo to spring into action. On the verge of going manic she throws herself into the fight.
