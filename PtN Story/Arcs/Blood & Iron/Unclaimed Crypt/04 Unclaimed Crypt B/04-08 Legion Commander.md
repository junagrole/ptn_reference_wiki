Bickering with [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] about [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] gets [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Horo|Horo]] to open up about her commander. Before they became [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] they were a mercenary fleet, taking commissions and avoiding fights. 
They lacked the strength to take on the injustice they encountered and experienced. [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl]] invited Zoya to increase their fighting power, shattering Horos self confidence.

Trying to prove herself Horo picked fights with gangsters and duelled Zoya. On one such excursion she got infected by Mania. Seeking one last fight she challenged Zoya again, who beat her into the ground before encouraging her not to give up. 
Horo came out of that ordeal as a [[Topics/Mania/Sinner|Sinner]] and with boundless admiration for her boss.

Horo picks up Chiefs unease about willingness to follow Zoya anywhere right away. But to her Chief is exactly the type of person that Zoya would like. 

The heart to hearts gets interrupted by a ragtag bunch of mania addled gang members that caught up with the team.
