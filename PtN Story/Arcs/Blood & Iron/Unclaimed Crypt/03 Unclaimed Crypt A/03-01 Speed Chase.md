Pursued by [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ninety-Nine|Ninety-Nine]] on a motorbike the group can only speed down the streets of Syndicate to escape. With [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Che|Che]] driving [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] speculate why the Legion is after them.
The argument and the escape are cut short by a swarm of gangsters fleeing in the opposite direction.

While fighting off the gangsters intent on seizing your car Hella is nearly hit by a grenade. Only a sudden command from the shackles allows her to dodge. While still cursing the Chief out her attitude has softened considerably.

Meanwhile Ninety-Nine and Legion members catch up with the group. While the Legion merely intents to kidnap Chief for their boss the enraged Ninety-Nine is set on murder.
Furious about her using the shackles on Hella she launches a deadly assault on Chief.

With the encirclement thrown in disarray the group takes the chance to escape by car once more.
