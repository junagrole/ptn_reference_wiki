Luring out the [[Organisations/Gangs/Yagyu|Yagyu]] gang members, Chief pretends to be a newbie Sheriff working with Ted that got taken by [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] after the battle at [[Locations/DisCity/West District/SALVA Hospital|SALVA Hospital]].

Thinking he landed an intelligence windfall the Yagyu Executive offers Chief an alliance. Revealing that the gang alliance set up a trap for [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] in the underground levels and that Chiefs target, the Legion Sinner who escaped from SALVA is already trapped half dead underground, too.

The Yagyu offer to hide Chief from the Legion by staging a kidnapping, so Earl won't have a reason to set off the bomb. Seeing Chief as a valuable talent for training Sinners, as evidenced by [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]]. Yagyu is training young Sinners as obedient child soldiers, like the [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/SALVA/Gray-haired Girl|Gray-haired Girl]] they sold to [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ted|Ted]]. Apparently Ted was a pioneer in brainwashing and abusing kids.

To Yagyu young Sinners are but superior versions of mania weapons. An enraged Chief confronts the Executive about just wanting her Sinners. Having reached her limits the team drops the deception.
