With the car destroyed and [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] being trapped by the wreckage [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] has to face [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] assault on her own. While Hecate refuses an offer to join the Legion Hella crawls out of the wreckage. Furious over the drivers presumed death she kicks of the fight.

Just then [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ninety-Nine|Ninety-Nine]] joins the fray, only to be stopped by Hella. Before any more pursuers can catch up the unexpectedly alive [[Characters/DisCity/Inner Bay/Che|Che]] snatches one of the Legions cars.
Only now, with Ninenty-Nines helmet destroyed, can Hella recognise her friend as the car speeds away.

Once again their escape is cut short by an ambush. This time it's [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] herself, sending the approaching car flying with a single punch. Their luck finally having run out, the group gets captured.
