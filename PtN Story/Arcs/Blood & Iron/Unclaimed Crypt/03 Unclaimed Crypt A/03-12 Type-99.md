Disregarding [[Hecate]] calling for a retreat [[Hella]] rushes ahead into a concealed laboratory filled with more Type-99 marked jars. Shattered by another pulse from the [[Inheritance]] their contamination pools together and forms a polymer in the form of [[Ninety-Nine]].
The sight of her friend leaves Hella absolutely shocked. [[Parma]] used 99 as a cultivation medium for the mania crystals forged into [[Mania Weapon]]. 

The mania weapon enable their wielder to use the power of the [[Sinner]] they were extracted from. Hella wants revenge on those responsible for torturing her, 99 and the other Sinners, but can only vent her rage on the walls.

The influence of the Inheritance not only caused the mania crystals to go manic, even the elevator shaft itself is filled with contamination. To prevent an even worse calamity Chief decides to press on despite the danger.
