While slowly advancing into the gang occupied building complex [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl]] gives Chief a short history lesson about the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|West District]]. The conversation is cut short by a contamination trap.

The trap was not set by Sinners, but by [[Organisations/Gangs/Thou Voodoo]] gang members wielding [[Mania Weapon]]s. Coordinating with them against [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion]] a group of [[Organisations/Gangs/Gonzales]] members flank the MBCC team. 

Before reinforcements from the [[Organisations/Gangs/Red Falcon]] and [[Organisations/Gangs/Carnivore]] can join the fray they get taken out by [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya]] and her Legionaries. To oppose Zoya the rival gangs unite for the first time, but that only gives the Legion the chance for a decisive strike.

Meanwhile [[Hecate]] is mostly worried about the unknown mania weapons the gangsters wield.
