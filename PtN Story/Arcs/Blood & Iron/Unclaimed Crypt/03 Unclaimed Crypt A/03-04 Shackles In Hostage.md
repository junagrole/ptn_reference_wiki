[[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion|The Legion]] convoy arrives at the [[Locations/DisCity/West District/Crypt|Crypt]] to claim the [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]]. While the excavation site of the crypt is fortified by the remaining major Syndicate gangs [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl]] is more worried about the mania contamination inside the crypt. But [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] forces the reluctant MBCC Chief to shackle her, so she can withstand the intense mania field. Only seeing Hecate and Hella threatened does Chief give in to her demands.

Wanting to test how the Shackles affect her abilities she throws a car at a group of gang members waiting in ambush and crushes them in a short fight. [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] is worried about the side effects of shackling an [[Topics/Mania/Sinner|S class Sinner]], but Chief reassures her that this will work in their favour eventually.  

Earl overhearing them tries to reason with them. The Legion is trying to unify [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicate]], so they don't intend to make unnecessary enemies of the authorities. Seeing Chief reluctant to make a deal with the Sinner who interrogated Hecate and Hella, he resorts to the threat of his ability. Using a mania bomb inside Chiefs head as leverage Earl makes clear that he disapproves of this partnership and will terminate it permanently once she is no longer useful to Zoya.

[[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] unsuccessfully tries to get information about [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Ninety-Nine|Ninety-Nine]] out of Earl, but is cut short by Zoya giving the order to move out. Leaving Chief in a lose lose situation.
