Realising Chief played them, the [[Organisations/Gangs/Yagyu|Yagyu]] Executive calls for his allies from [[Organisations/Gangs/Thou Voodoo|Thou Voodoo]] and [[Organisations/Gangs/Carnivore|Carnivore]] that lie in wait, all armed with [[Topics/Mania/Mania Weapon|Mania Weapon]]. Losing his composure he shows clear signs of Mania contamination.

The overuse of [[Topics/Mania/Shackles|Potestas]] and the close proximity of the [[Topics/Mania/Inheritance|Inheritance]] cause Chief to lose focus. [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hecate|Hecate]] urges [[Characters/Paradeisos/MBCC/Hella|Hella]] to evacuate her, while she holds of the enemy. But both Hella and Chief are vehemently against the plan, making Chief flat our order Hecate to stay at their side.

Getting cornered by the combined reinforcements of the allied gangs the MBCC team fulfilled their role as bait. After crushing the [[Organisations/Gangs/Red Falcon|Red Falcon]] detachment baiting her, [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] and her subordinates attack the now concentrated gang forces.

Faced by her overwhelming power the Yagyu executive begs for his life, claiming he was forced into the gang by circumstances. But his extravagant sword is proof that he killed scores of people to get his position. He pledges his loyalty to her as the new overlord of [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District|Syndicate]] in a final bid to save his life.

But Zoya doesn't want to rule Syndicate, she wants to crush it and every single gang member along with it.
> [!cite] Zoya
> I don't want to be your king. I want your death.

Having killed her prey out of fury, she commands her Legionaries to grill the remaining members for info on the situation before killing them, too.
