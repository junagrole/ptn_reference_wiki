Reaching the central elevator to the underground levels [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya|Zoya]] plans to only take the MBCC team with her, leaving [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Earl|Earl]] and her underlings to destroy the massive arsenal of [[Topics/Mania/Mania Weapon|Mania Weapon]] stashed at the elevator. 

Chief uses the sudden ambush of [[Organisations/Gangs/Red Falcon|Red Falcon]] gangsters, who detonate the stored mania weapons as a chance to get away from Zoya. 
The plan is simple, intentional get taken as hostage by one of the surviving [[Organisations/Gangs/Yagyu|Yagyu]] members. Zoya refuses to intervene and Chief gets dragged away into a hidden passage.
