>[!info] Background
>Born into a prestigious medical family in [[Locations/DisCity/Eastside/Eastside]], Iron graduates from [[Organisations/Academia/Banyan Medical College]] as a neurosurgeon. She refused all offers and went to treat [[Mania]] in [[Locations/DisCity/West District/West District]].  
>While she kept contact with [[Characters/DisCity/Syndicate/Zoya]] and [[Organisations/Gangs/The Legion]] she established a truly neutral clinic not beholden to any gang.  
>During her duties she became infected with Mania as well. At a dark point it was only [[Anne]], herself suffering from the infection begging her for help that gave her a reason to live on.  
>To treat her she cut off her own arm to slow the spread of the infection, awakening her as a Sinner.
