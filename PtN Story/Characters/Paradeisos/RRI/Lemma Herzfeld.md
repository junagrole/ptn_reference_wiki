**Lemma Herzfeld** is a scientist at the [[Ring Research Institute]], heading the [[BR003]] Observation Hub. Born in NF 98/99 she was selected by the Heterogeneous Trial program and received training in [[Paradeisos]] under the mentorship of [[EDGE 02]].

In May NF 113 she was appointed to the EDGE 07 seat after the High Tower black ring broke out. 36 months into her tenure she was removed from her seat by EDGE 02 and Director Isomer. A few days later she reclaimed her seat with the assistance of [[Chief|SHP-13]], Summer and her researcher team. By her demand she became the designated EDGE 02 backup and took over the Heterogeneous Trial project.
