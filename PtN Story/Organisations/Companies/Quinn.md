Founded in N.F. 104 by [[Eirene Campbell]] it quickly grew into one of the biggest companies in DisCity. Quinn is focused on [[Sandship]] construction, [[Outland]] logistics and entertainment. For several years Quinn has tried to enter the [[Hypercube]] trade, but [[Nirvana]] has managed to defend their monopoly.
