The Exploration, Defence and Governance Entente was drafted by a group of visionaries lead by [[Characters/Paradeisos/DisSea Expedition/Keylan|Keylan]]. It established [[Locations/DisCity/DisCity|DisCity]] as a mining city on the shores of [[Locations/DisSea/DisSea|DisSea]]. The city would mine and export [[Topics/Reason/Hypercube|Hypercubes]] and receive goods and other supports from the city states.

One after another the surviving city states joined the Entente.
