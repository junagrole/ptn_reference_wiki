The West District was DisCities industrial centre and the main hub for hypercube mining and processing. But most mines were destroyed in the [[DisSea explosion]] and the West District subsequently abandoned by the rest of the city. The separation was formalised by the [[West District Management Regulations]] in N.F. 99 and the now gang ruled district became known as Syndicate.
